01 / ABOUT

A little about me

Computing without compromise.

I’m an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at NYU Tandon. I lead the Brooklyn Application, Architecture, and Hardware Lab (BAAHL).

My research sits at the intersection of computer architecture, cryptography, and machine learning. We build the specialized processors, compilers, and systems required to make secure computation efficient enough for real-world use.

Previously, I worked on AI infrastructure at Facebook. I received my PhD from Harvard University and undergraduate degrees from UMass Amherst.
