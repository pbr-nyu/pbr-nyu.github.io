Brandon Reagen

Associate Professor, Electrical and Computer Engineering

New York University · Tandon School of Engineering

My research focuses on computer architecture and hardware–software systems that make privacy-preserving computing and machine learning practical.

I direct the Brooklyn Application, Architecture, and Hardware Lab (BAAHL) at NYU. Our work spans specialized cryptographic accelerators, private machine learning, zero-knowledge proofs, and efficient computing systems.

Research

More about our work →

We investigate the hardware and system foundations of efficient secure computation. Current interests include:

  • Hardware and compiler co-design for fully homomorphic encryption and private AI
  • Zero-knowledge proving systems and accelerators
  • Computer architecture for emerging workloads and energy-efficient computing
