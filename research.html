Research

Computer architecture and hardware–software co-design for private, efficient computing.

Our research connects algorithms, compilers, systems, and hardware to remove performance barriers to secure computation.

Research Areas

  • Privacy-preserving machine learning.

    Efficient systems and optimizations for machine learning on sensitive or encrypted data.

  • Cryptographic hardware acceleration.

    Specialized architectures for homomorphic encryption, garbled circuits, and related primitives.

  • Zero-knowledge proofs.

    Systems and accelerators that reduce the computational cost of generating proofs.

  • Computer architecture.

    Hardware/software co-design for emerging and data-intensive computing workloads.
