Research

Building systems that make private and efficient computing practical.

Our group works across computer architecture, hardware accelerators, compilers, and privacy-preserving machine learning.

Research areas

  • Hardware acceleration for cryptography

    Architectures that accelerate fully homomorphic encryption, zero-knowledge proofs, and secure computation.

  • Private machine learning

    Efficient systems and compilers for inference on encrypted and sensitive data.

  • Computer architecture and systems

    Hardware–software co-design for emerging computing workloads.
